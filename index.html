Key terms
r Radius — distance from centre to any point on boundary
d Diameter — straight line through centre, edge to edge. d = 2r
C Circumference — total length of the circle's boundary (perimeter)
A Area — 2-D space enclosed by the circle (always cm²)
π Pi — ratio of circumference to diameter. A constant ≈ 3.14159…

Key reminders
Perimeter of partial circles always includes the straight edge(s) — don't just find the arc!
Semicircle has 1 straight edge = diameter (d)
Quadrant has 2 straight edges = 2 radii = 1 diameter (d)
Sector (θ°) has 2 straight edges = 2 radii = 1 diameter (d)
Area units are always cm² — perimeter units are cm
Compound shapes: split into parts, calculate each, then add or subtract
Convert between r and d before substituting into any formula
Partial circle shapes
Semicircle
P: ½πd + d
A: ½πr²
Quadrant
P: ¼πd + d
A: ¼πr²
Any sector
P: θ/360·πd + d
A: θ/360·πr²
⚠ Perimeter trap: The "+d" term (straight edges) is the most common mistake. Always ask: how many straight edges does this shape have?
